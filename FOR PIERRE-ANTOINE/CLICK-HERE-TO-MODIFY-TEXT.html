✎ Modify the site's text

This page opens the LIVE files with the pencil editor available. The published website never shows the pencil — it only exists on your computer.

How it works
  1. Click a page below.
  2. Click the ✎ pencil button (bottom-left) on that page.
  3. Click any text and retype it in your own words.
  4. Hit “Export changes” — a file lands in your Downloads.
  5. Tell any Claude chat: “apply my edits”. Done.
