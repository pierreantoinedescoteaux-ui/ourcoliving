# Ecovillage

> Ecovillage

_A whole settlement designed around living lightly — food, energy, governance, everything._

## What it is   <!-- body -->

The maximalist answer. An ecovillage doesn't just share a kitchen — it rebuilds the entire stack of daily life around regeneration: growing food, producing energy, consensus governance, often its own economy.

It's also the deepest commitment on this map. The successful ones took decades and survived on stubbornness. But nothing else produces the same depth of belonging.

## Who it suits   <!-- who -->

People ready to make community the main project of their life, not a feature of it.

## What to watch for   <!-- watch -->

Consensus fatigue is real. The governance load is a part-time job.

## Real examples   <!-- examples -->

### Findhorn   <!-- examples[0] -->

The 60-year proof of concept — from caravan park to full eco-settlement.

### Auroville   <!-- examples[1] -->

The most ambitious version ever attempted — a planned universal township.

### EcoVillage Ithaca   <!-- examples[2] -->

The polished North American model: three cohousing neighborhoods on shared land.

### Tamera   <!-- examples[3] -->

A 200-person peace-research ecovillage that re-watered its own valley — one of Europe's most complete intentional communities.
