# about.html — mobile pass report

## Done
- Forced `.reveal` sections to permanent `opacity:1; transform:none` on mobile — the phone capture showed the quote card followed by ~2240px of blank cream (crops confirmed `.trans`, `.bring`, `.doors`, `.contact` never rendered) because the scroll-triggered fade-in never flipped to `.in`; now everything below the hero is guaranteed visible regardless of IntersectionObserver timing. Visible: screens 2 through 5 of the scroll (everything after the pull-quote card) — "what I bring" grid, the two door cards, and the email CTA all now show up where before they were just cream background.
- Bumped `.thread-body`, `.trans .t-body`, `.bcard .b-line`, `.door .d-desc`, `.contact .c-sub` to the playbook's fluid body formula (16→18px) — at 390px their `clamp()` was resolving to the vw-starved minimum (14.7–15.2px), under the 16px reading floor. Visible: the pull-quote caption, the "said plainly" callout body, all 4 "what I bring" card lines, both door-card descriptions, and the CTA sub-line.
- Bumped `.eyebrow`, `.trans .kicker`, `.bcard .b-theme`, `.door .d-link` from .62–.72rem (9.9–11.5px) to .75rem (12px) to clear the micro-label floor. Visible: "ABOUT PIERRE-ANTOINE" eyebrow (screen 1), "SAID PLAINLY" kicker and the 4 card theme labels ("Communities that outlive me" etc.) and door-card "Read the story →" / "See the work →" links (screen 5, only visible once fix #1 landed).
- Made `.btn-pill` (the email CTA) wrap instead of overflowing — `report.json` flagged it at `right:545` against a 390px viewport (~155px off-canvas, clipped by the site's `overflow-x:clip`). Added `max-width:100%; overflow-wrap:anywhere; word-break:break-word; text-align:center` so the full email is readable across 2 lines. Visible: last screen, the "Say hello" CTA button.
- Added `overflow-wrap:break-word` to the 5 headline classes (hero h1, thread quote, transparency line, contact line, door titles) as a defensive guard against forced horizontal scroll — no visual change today, just a safety net.
- Gave the "what I bring" card proof-links (`.bcard a` — "That story", "Design for Connection", etc.) real tap padding; they were bare inline anchors with only a 10px top margin from an inline style, well under the 44px tap-target floor. Visible: screen 4, under each of the 4 "what I bring" cards.

## Proposed (not done)
- The invisible-reveal bug is fixed defensively via CSS, but the root cause (IntersectionObserver never firing for below-the-fold `.reveal` sections during the audit's programmatic scroll) is a JS/timing issue in the shared reveal script in `about.html`'s own `<script>` block — worth a real fix there so desktop/tablet don't rely on the same silent failure mode. Out of scope for this CSS-only pass.
- Hero grid's `portrait-col{order:-1}` (image before text) already lives in the page's own `<style>` at a 720px breakpoint — inconsistent with the 760px sitewide cutoff, so a device between 721–760px wide would miss it. Low-risk, low-value edge case; flagging rather than patching since it'd mean re-declaring the whole grid rule outside the page's existing breakpoint scheme.

## Flags
- `div.wmedia` / `img` / `video` overflow reported in `report.json` (left:-12, right:402) comes from the shared `site-nav.js` footer parallax band (`inset:-7% -3%` by design), not from anything in `about.html` itself — intentional full-bleed effect, contained by `overflow-x:clip` on `html,body`. Not a real bug, not touched (shared component, out of this page's scope).
- The invisible-reveal issue (see Proposed) means the *baseline screenshot itself* undersold this page's real problem — anyone reviewing only the screenshot before this fix would've seen a page that's ~50% blank cream space with no visible "what I bring" grid, door cards, or contact CTA.
