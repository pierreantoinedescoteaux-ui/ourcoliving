# Co-living Portfolio — Link Bank

## What this file is
A persistent reference bank of links collected during research and browsing for this site.
Future sessions: retrieve entries with `grep` on the tag fields below.

## How to add an entry
Paste under the right category heading. Use the template:

```
- **Name** — [Project/article name]
  URL: <url>
  Tags: type:[slug] · use:[slug] · status:[slug]
  Description: One sentence — what it is + why it's here.
  Photos: [yes — images/filename.jpg | no]
```

**Controlled vocab**
- type: `ecovillage` `cohousing` `housing-coop` `baugruppen` `kibbutz` `intergenerational` `hacker-house` `operator-coliving` `entrepreneur-house` `rural-coliving` `network-village` `student-coop` `city-scale` `care`
- use: `example` `article` `inspiration` `photo-source` `book`
- status: `integrated` (already in site) · `pending` (candidate for integration) · `reference-only` (context/reading, not a site example)

## How to retrieve
- All pending examples: `grep "status:pending"`
- All kibbutz links: `grep "type:kibbutz"`
- All articles: `grep "use:article"`
- Links with photos saved: `grep "Photos: yes"`
