# Voice, tone and values: read before writing ANY copy for this site

Written 2026-10-20 from P-A's own words. Every headline, intro, caption, button and
blurb follows this. When in doubt, read P-A's own lines (bottom) and sound like them.

## What the site is
An artistic, visual picture of P-A's hope for the future: a dream tower where each
room opens onto resources about coliving, design and building a better future.
It is an **invitation**, not a portfolio pitch. "This is something we build together."

## The voice
- **Warm, inviting, human.** Talk to one person, like a host welcoming a guest.
- **Humble.** P-A has NOT figured it out. He is learning, in the open, with others.
- **Collaborative.** "We", "together", "come help", "tell me what's missing".
  The work belongs to the people in it, not to P-A.
- **Earnest and grounded.** Direct and plain. From the heart, but never over-the-top
  poetic. Say what happened and what it meant.
- **A little imaginative.** Lean gently on the tower and its rooms (the summit, the
  garden where we grow hope, a seat at the long table). One image per line, not five.

## Don't
- **Self-made-hero framing.** e.g. "I created what I wished existed for myself", "Said
  plainly", "nobody assigned them to me". It centres P-A alone; his values are collaborative.
- **Jokes that undercut** ("zero permits", "a word I use loosely", "a sentence I never
  expected to write", "made a lasting peace with spreadsheets"). One light touch per section, max.
- **Lines that push the reader away** ("the short version, because you have things to do").
- **LinkedIn / sales speak** ("receipts instead of adjectives", "what I bring to a team",
  "zero-to-one", "credential").
- **Slogans** ("Community as the antidote to isolation, space as the tool that makes it stick").
- **AI tells:** long dashes, "not X, but Y" / "not a straight line, an exploration",
  lists of three, vague headlines that say nothing ("I explored my way here").
- **Overclaiming** ("everything on this site", "world-class").

## Do
- Plain first-person sentences. Concrete details beat adjectives (stock-photo ads,
  fifty strangers interviewed for a house that didn't exist yet).
- End sections with an opening for the reader: a question, an invitation, a seat.
- Admit what's unfinished ("This room is still being built").

## Facts
- Never publish an unconfirmed number or claim. Numbers awaiting P-A's OK: see the
  "Story + About" tab of the copy sheet (row 31).
- Nothing internal on the live site: no `[edit]` markers, no "photo wanted" text.

## P-A's own lines (the reference voice)
- "This site aims to centre coliving as a means to a healthier, more sustainable and
  communal future. This tower is an artistic representation of my hopes. ... Hope you enjoy your stay."
- "The garden is where we grow hope."
- "Building villages in community. Find yours."
- "The commons is a space for collaboration and community."
- "Take me to the top."
